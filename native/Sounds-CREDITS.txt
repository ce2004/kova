TailRemote's event sounds

Piano: Steinway concert grand recordings from the University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios, Musical Instrument Samples (theremin.music.uiowa.edu). Free to use for any project, without restriction.

Harp, glockenspiel, marimba, xylophone, violin section pizzicato and Nepalese bells: Versilian Studios Chamber Orchestra 2, Community Edition (github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE), released into the public domain under CC0 1.0.

Tones and beeps (a beep, a chirp, a swoop, a pop, a ding and the rest): made by Kova itself, in code.

Classic phone sounds: made by Kova itself, in the style of early monophonic phones. Gran Vals is by Francisco Tarrega (1902) and is in the public domain.
